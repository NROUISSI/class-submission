The nine stages

Hard rules of a reading

  • A frame card comes first.
  • See the system before making solutions.
  • People before prototypes.
  • Every make card is followed by a test card.
  • Evidence before scale.
  • An ethics card whenever the problem is wicked (2 or more out of 3).
  • At least half the cards come from the crux rows. Every crux gets 2 or more cards.
  • Every round deals at least 9 cards. There is no ceiling.
  • Never mark a card done if its output is thin.

Three rounds

  • Round 1 · Understand (stages 1–4): problem, stakeholders, real field data, insights and a How might we. A wicked problem needs field data from two different cards.
  • Round 2 · Create (stages 5–7): ideas, a concept, a prototype, evidence and assumptions, plus an ethics check.
  • Round 3 · Grow (stages 7–8): a plan, a coalition or business model, and ethics. Only once there is test evidence.
